# People Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Execute one task at a time and keep the migration explicit and owner-scoped.

**Goal:** Deliver a private, persisted People directory with search, editable contact details, and archive/restore.

**Architecture:** Clerk-authenticated Hono routes validate shared Zod schemas and call owner-scoped Drizzle repositories. React queries those same-origin routes and renders the directory. People-to-event associations follow as a separate increment after this directory is usable.

**Tech Stack:** Existing React, TypeScript, Hono, Zod, Drizzle, Neon, TanStack Query, Vitest, Cloudflare Workers.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-people-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Do not trust owner IDs from the browser.
- Keep birthdays as PostgreSQL `date` values.
- Do not accept photos or expose contacts outside their owner.
- Keep migrations explicit; never migrate on a request.
